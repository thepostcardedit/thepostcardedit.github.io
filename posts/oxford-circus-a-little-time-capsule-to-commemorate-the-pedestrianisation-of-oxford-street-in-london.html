The Postcard Edit: One Perfect Time Capsule

📍 Oxford Circus, London

London is always changing, isn’t it? We went Christmas shopping on Saturday and ended up in H&M to have a look at the Elie Saab x H&M holiday and winter collection (that’s another post entirely).

I was looking out of the window when I realised that was probably the last time I’d ever see the Oxford Circus diagonal crossing, so I went and snapped a few pictures from one of the top floors.

Imagine looking back at this post in ten years’ time and seeing Oxford Circus as it was in October 2026, just as London was beginning to make more room for people…!!

Save this for your next trip round and round.
