The Liberty Christmas shop is OPEN 🎄✨

A look inside Liberty’s Christmas shop, with all the decorations and festive details that make this time of year oh, so lovely, plus a random encounter in the pre-dawn sky

The Postcard Edit: One Perfect Christmas

📍 Liberty Christmas Shop, Regent Street, London

Not sure what’s going on here but this year I’m getting ridiculously excited about Christmas!!

I went shopping at the weekend. Carnaby Street was so busy that they had airport style queuing outside the shops. Is this normal?? Do I live under a rock??

Anyway, I stopped for a coffee at EL&N and eventually ended up in Liberty of London, mostly because I wanted to take pictures of the London mural and their staircase, and ended up in…

(Drumroll)

The annual Liberty Christmas shop!!

Now I know this is difficult to believe, but I arrived during a rare quiet moment, so I was able to spend quite a lot of time taking pictures just by myself

If you’re curious about what’s trending in Christmas decorations, then look no further, the answer is… literally everything from hummingbirds to mice, to moons and owls, to eyes (!!!), to even Barbie Christmas ornaments!!

Speaking of moons, I’ve spent every morning this past week looking toward the eastern sky to admire the Leo constellation high up above, and to my surprise, I kept seeing the cosmic kiss between Jupiter and Mars!! I believe this is still happening, so if you’re up at 5 AM (yes, I am one of those) then look up!! The sky is beautiful this time of the year✨🌌🪐
