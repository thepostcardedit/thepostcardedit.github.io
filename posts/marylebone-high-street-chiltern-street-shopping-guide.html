Independent shops and seasonal displays around Marylebone High Street and Chiltern Street.

Discover the loveliest shops in Marylebone with beautiful shopfronts, seasonal decorations and thoughtful gift ideas for the person who has everything

The Postcard Edit: One Perfect Shopping Spree

📍 Marylebone, London

Autumn and Winter!! As they say, there is a fine line, especially at this time of the year when London is a mix of Halloween, Christmas, and everything in between. It’s so much fun to capture the vibe!!

And on that note, I get so many questions from people who are after gift ideas. What do you buy for the person who seems to have it all?? I always answer the same: buy independent. You can never go wrong with independent!!

I went onto a rather lovely walk to take pictures of my favourite moments, mostly on Chiltern St., which I haven’t really been to since the big fire, so I took the opportunity to check how the works on Chiltern Firehouse are going

My walk brought back really good memories. I love it there. I even celebrated my birthday there “a few” years ago, which is a story for another time, but wow, how has so much time passed? I had to scroll an embarrassingly long way down my own feed to find the post. Anyway. It’s not really the kind of place where you can take your phone out and start recording but they were OK with me taking a couple close-up pictures of the food, then the phone went away (revolutionary behaviour on this platform)

If you’d like to recreate my walk, these are the places in my carousel: Anna & Nina, At The Movies, Cologne & Cotton, Websters, Bella Freud, Perfumer H, Monc, Grandirosa, Anatome, Casely Hayford, Velasca, Grey Flannel, Veronica Beard, Gallery Eclectic, Kayo Suzuki (Tokyo-based ceramic artist) and finally, Selfridges which is already decked out for Christmas!!

Save this if my London adventures inspire your own 👸🏼
