Boodles and Biscuiteers 🍪 💎

THE POSTCARD EDIT: ONE PERFECT BITE · LONDON
The pink Boodles and Biscuiteers cart outside the new Boodles flagship on Old Bond Street.

Boodles has teamed up with Biscuiteers to celebrate the opening of The Boodles Townhouse with tea and biscuits

The Postcard Edit: One Perfect Bite

📍 The Boodles Townhouse 23 Old Bond Street Mayfair London

The prettiest pink cart was parked outside Boodles’ new London flagship, literally across the road from its former home opposite Tiffany’s (where Stella McCartney used to be). There were so many people milling about, and between the crowds and the dreaded white van, I couldn’t take a decent picture of the building 😭

Thankfully we were gifted lots of nice things so I was able to take pictures of all that, although I had a small biscuit-related mishap when one took a tumble and broke in half (naturally, I decided to put it to good use)

The chaos turned out to be a blessing in disguise, because as I was queuing someone from Boodles appeared and asked a few of us if we’d like to step into The Boodles Townhouse for a viewing and before long, we were being ushered through the doors. We were warned that what whatever happens in Boodles stays in Boodles, cameras firmly away, so you’ll have to take my word for it that this place is stunning!!

The Boodles Townhouse has seven floors (yes, you read that right) with themed rooms, all connected via the coolest spiral staircase I’ve ever seen which was designed by Stella McCartney! But I won’t bore you with details (mostly because I don’t really remember. Did I mention there are seven floors in this place??) so let’s just list the things that stroke me the most and that I am allowed to tell you about…

The pink marble flooring, the Raindance Bar (bars are really not my thing but this one is a work of art), the Secret Garden designed by RHS Chelsea Flower Show gold medallist Catherine MacDonald aaaand the tree!! Yes, they have a massive Japanese acer tree inside the store!!

Save this before the biscuit cart disappears 🍪✨
