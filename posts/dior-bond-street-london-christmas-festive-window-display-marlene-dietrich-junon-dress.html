Dior’s spectacular Christmas window displays have arrived on New Bond Street in London, featuring a miniature Paris inspired by 30 Avenue Montaigne

The Postcard Edit: One Perfect Christmas

📍 Dior London flagship boutique Bond St. London

The Christmas lights hadn’t even been switched on at the time of writing this, but as always, Dior got ahead of the curve!!

If you’re a fashion and art and film geek like I am… actually, if you’re a geek like I am, then you’ll love the story behind…

Set in 1949, it mirrors Dior’s 2026 animated holiday short film, Christian Dior’s Night in Colour (directed by Sylvain Chomet and creative-directed by Jonathan Anderson) which celebrates the famous real-life friendship between Christian Dior and Marlene Dietrich

In the film, Marlene Dietrich calls Christian Dior with a fashion emergency but what could it be?

Her director has suddenly decided to shoot in colour!!

Oh no!!

Thankfully, Dior’s artisans work through the night to build a gown for her (the 1949 Junon dress) and save the day…!!

I went analog for this one!! Can you see the difference in the window reflections (or lack of thereof???) sadly when I tried with my phone, the pictures and videos didn’t come out great

I get the feeling Dior is going to be the most popular window on Bond, St. this Christmas. There are two other doll houses so stay tuned, I might post them later!!

Save this if you love Dior, on the big screen, and in colour
