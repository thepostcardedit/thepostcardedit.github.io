About The Postcard Edit

Pretty pictures of the mundane.

The Postcard Edit began in 2013 as a visual project documenting the beauty in everyday life: the quiet, the ordinary and the seemingly mundane. Over time, it grew into a recognisable creative identity, with a distinct aesthetic centred on simplicity, stillness and the details that might otherwise go unnoticed.

The idea has always been to look a little closer. To notice the small moments, places and details that stay with you, whether that's a corner of London, somewhere new to explore or something unexpectedly lovely in an otherwise ordinary day.

The Original Postcard Edit

The Postcard Edit was established in 2013 and is the original creator and originator of the name. This website documents that history and provides a home for my original work. Accounts, profiles or businesses using the same or a similar name that appeared later are separate from the original project.
