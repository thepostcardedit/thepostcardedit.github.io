About

The Postcard Edit began in 2013 as a personal collection of places, details and experiences worth remembering. From London cafés and quiet walks to pop-ups, exhibitions and small everyday discoveries, this is where I share the things that catch my eye.

Think of it as a living scrapbook of London and beyond: part visual journal, part collection of recommendations, and part record of the places and moments I want to keep.
